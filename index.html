About关于

I am a master's student in computer science at Northwestern University, working with Prof. Xiao Luo (UW–Madison) and Prof. Hang Zhou (UNC–Chapel Hill). I received my B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Wisconsin–Madison in May 2026.

I want language agents to be useful well beyond the few places they work today. A model that can read and reason should be able to act: in a browser, in a kitchen, on a live video stream, beside a doctor reading a scan. Most of what stops it is practical: too little memory, too little compute, too little time. I work on removing those obstacles one at a time, so that agents can be taken into more domains.

我目前在西北大学攻读计算机科学硕士，与 Xiao Luo 教授（威斯康星大学麦迪逊分校）和 Hang Zhou 教授（北卡罗来纳大学教堂山分校）合作。2026 年 5 月，我从威斯康星大学麦迪逊分校本科毕业，获计算机科学学士学位。

我希望语言智能体能走出今天这几个少数场景，真正派上用场。一个能阅读、能推理的模型，也应该能行动：在浏览器里，在厨房里，在实时视频流上，在读片的医生身边。挡住它的多半是现实的限制：记忆太少，算力太少，时间太少。我做的事，是把这些障碍一个一个拿掉，让智能体走进更多领域。

Research研究

Agents stay out of most places they could help because they need large models, spend compute freely, and cannot keep up with a world that does not pause. My recent work takes these in turn.智能体进不了许多本可以帮上忙的地方，是因为它们离不开大模型，花算力毫无节制，也跟不上一个从不暂停的世界。我最近的工作依次处理这几件事。

Small models小模型

Small agents lose track of what happened only a few steps ago. This work asks how they can carry what matters forward on their own.小型智能体会忘掉仅仅几步之前发生的事。这项工作关心的是，它们如何靠自己把重要的东西带下去。

Beyond the History Window: Window-Informed Harness Distillation for Small LLM AgentsIn preparation.撰写中。

Limited compute有限算力

Reasoning models spend very different amounts of thought on different questions. This work is about spending a fixed budget of thinking where it helps most.推理模型在不同问题上花的思考量差别很大。这项工作关心的是，如何把有限的思考预算花在最有用的地方。

Opening-oriented Cost Regression with Multi-student Probing for LLM Thinking Budget ControlUnder review.审稿中。

Real time实时

A model watching a live stream cannot keep every frame, and cannot wait for the end before it speaks. This work is about understanding video as it arrives.看实时视频流的模型存不下每一帧，也不能等到结束才开口。这项工作关心的是，如何在视频到来的同时理解它。

CENTRE: Calibrated Response Trigger with Multi-resolution Retention for Online Video UnderstandingUnder review.审稿中。
